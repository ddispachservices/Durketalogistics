Questions answered

Frequently Asked Questions

A straightforward look at how we work with owner-operators and small fleets.

What does a dispatcher do?

A dispatcher finds loads, negotiates rates with brokers, handles communication and manages the paperwork so the driver can focus on driving.

Do carriers have to take every load you find?

No. You approve every load before we book it.

How much do you charge?

Our fee is 5% of the load rate. There is no setup fee and no hidden charges.

What truck types do you dispatch?

Dry van, reefer, flatbed, step deck, power only, box truck, hot shot and sprinter van. Availability can vary, so tell us what you run.

Do you handle paperwork?

Yes. We help with rate confirmations, BOLs, broker setup packets and invoicing support.

Do you help with detention and TONU?

Yes. We follow up with brokers on detention, layover and TONU situations when they apply.

Can I choose my own loads?

Yes. You have the final say on every load.

Do you work with small fleets?

Yes. We work with owner-operators and small fleets.
